01

ReCart.

One search.
Every marketplace.

Role / Product Designer + Front-endScope / End-to-endStatus / Working prototype
Problem

Secondhand shoppers jump between marketplaces with different filters, labels, and photography.

My move

I designed a unified browsing model, visual system, responsive behavior, and built it in React and Next.js.

Why it matters

The prototype makes scattered inventory feel comparable and gets people from search to decision faster.

Design decision

Normalize the mess before asking people to browse it.

A shared result model lets people compare price, condition, and source without learning each marketplace first.

My contribution: product model, visual system, responsive rules, and front-end implementation.
